End-to-End Planning for Autonomous Driving
DLAV Final Project · EgoDrivePlanner · Zhiyan Ke & Yanpeng Zhang
Data Preprocessing
Two Inputs: Camera + History Trajectory

The planner consumes a front-view RGB image and the ego vehicle's recent motion history. Raw dataset fields are transformed into a model-friendly representation before training.

Camera Branch

Raw frame shape is (200, 300, 3). Each image is resized to 224 x 336, converted to tensor, normalised, and batched as (B, 3, 224, 336).

Ego-Frame History

Raw history is (21, 3) with x, y, heading. It is recentered at the last pose and encoded as x, y, sin(theta), cos(theta), then augmented with velocity to form the GRU input.

Auxiliary Heads
Perception-Side Auxiliary Decoder
Feature fusion: layer3 spatial detail and layer4 semantics are projected to 128 channels, upsampled, and decoded jointly.
Two outputs: the decoder branches into a 15-class semantic head and a 1-channel depth head at image resolution.
Role in the model: these heads enrich the shared visual backbone with dense scene structure while the main planner still predicts the future ego trajectory.
Domain Adaptation
Closing the Sim-to-Real Gap

Models trained on synthetic sensor data fail on real-world data due to domain shift. Three complementary strategies are applied.

1
AdaIN style transfer Synthetic camera images are shifted toward real-image colour statistics using mean and std estimated from 200 real frames, with alpha = 0.5 blending.
2
Mixed-data training The training set combines 5,000 simulation samples with 500 labelled real samples so the planner sees both appearance domains.
3
Real-domain fine-tuning After mixed training, the entire network is fine-tuned for 20 epochs on real data to reduce the remaining deployment gap.
Architecture
EgoDrivePlanner

The model fuses visual context from a ResNet-34 backbone with a GRU-encoded motion history, then autoregressively predicts a 60-step future trajectory in the ego frame.
